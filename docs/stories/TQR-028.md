# TQR-028: Spoken overdue reminders on the kitchen screen

- Release: unreleased · Status: review · Owner: Claude (deliver-loop)
- Spec: S18 (AC60–AC64), S4 (house rules, AC12 versioning), S9 AC33 (late highlight), TQR-027 (voice)

## Story

As a cook, I want a dish that has waited too long said again ("Bàn 5 gọi phở bò đã 10 phút, chưa ra món") until it is out, so that no table is forgotten in a rush.

Owner (2026-11-08): "khi nào có ai đợi quá 5 hay 10 phút thì nhắc lại ví dụ bàn 5 gọi món xxx đã 10 phút r chưa ra đồ".

INVEST: Independent ☐ (needs TQR-027's voice) Negotiable ☑ Valuable ☑ Estimable ☑ Small ☑ Testable ☑ (pure scheduler; Playwright clock)

## Non-functional

- The rule is read as it is now (each queue read sends `reminder: {minutes, repeatMinutes}`), not from the order's AC12 snapshot: it is how the screen behaves, not a judgement of the order. Saving still versions rules exactly as S4 (`rules_updated_at` moves only on a real change).
- Tenant: the rule comes from the cook's own restaurant (session), never the request.

## Out of scope

- The waiter screen `/phuc-vu` (may later show an overdue count; it stays silent).
- Reminders for stations other than the one on screen.

## References

- Playwright `page.clock` (time travel in the browser while the server runs on real time).

## Design

- API: `house-rules.ts` gains `kitchen: {reminderMinutes, repeatMinutes}` (`REMINDER_MINUTES = [5, 10, 15, 20]`, defaults 10/10, filled on old rows by `readStoredRules`); `KitchenService.queue` adds `reminder` from the restaurant's current rules.
- Web: `voice/scheduler.ts` (`isOverdue`, `dueReminders`, `markSaid`), `phrases.ts` `reminderPhrase`; `useVoiceAnnouncements` checks on every clock tick (15 s, server-corrected); `TicketCard` takes `reminder` and shows "Trễ N phút"; settings page gets a "Nhắc món chờ lâu" section with two `RadioCardGroup`s.
