# TQR-013: Anomaly check, held orders, waiter confirm / reject

- Release: v0.3.0 · Status: review · Owner: Claude (agent, ordering branch)
- Spec: S8 (AC26–AC29), AC20, AC15, AC3; design.md (`anomaly.ts`), ADR 0005, `docs/design-system.md`

## Story

As a waiter, I want suspicious orders held for my confirmation, so that a table of one doesn't order for ten by mistake or prank — without the guest losing their place in the kitchen queue.

INVEST: Independent ☑ (after TQR-012) Negotiable ☑ Valuable ☑ Estimable ☑ Small ☑ Testable ☑

## Design

- Pure `services/api/src/service/domain/anomaly.ts`: `checkOrder({partySize, mainPortionsBefore, mainPortionsInOrder, isFirstOrder, rules}) → ok | flag-short | hold(reason)` (approval `none` never holds) and `ordersToRelease()` for AC20. Portions are counted from non-cancelled items, held ones included, since the waiter may yet confirm them.
- API: `GET /api/orders/review`, `POST /api/orders/:id/{confirm,reject,flag-seen}` (owner, cashier, waiter). Compare-and-set via `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` plus a status check.
- Web: `features/ordering/pages/ReviewPage.vue` (StaffShell, `/phuc-vu/duyet`, roles waiter/owner/cashier) and `ReviewCard.vue`: big "Xác nhận" / "Từ chối" (ConfirmSheet), "Đã xem" for flags, a polite status line; the list reloads on `order.status` from `/api/stream/floor`.
