# Changelog

Follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and [SemVer](https://semver.org/). One version for every deployable in this repo.

## [Unreleased]

### Security

- Production security headers (TQR-022): every API response — problem details and event streams included — sends a deny-all CSP, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `Referrer-Policy: same-origin`, `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin` and a `Permissions-Policy` denying camera, microphone and location; `X-Powered-By` is gone; `Strict-Transport-Security` with `TQR_HSTS=true`. The SPA's CSP and headers live in `deploy/security-headers.json`, are sent by `vite preview` and by the new `deploy/nginx.conf.example` (serves `web/dist`, proxies `/api`, SSE-ready), and every browser journey now fails on a CSP violation. New setting `TQR_TRUSTED_PROXIES` (Express `trust proxy`, default none): a client's own `X-Forwarded-For` no longer moves its rate-limit key, while a listed proxy's does.
- Step-up authentication for the bank account (TQR-023): changing or removing it via `PUT /api/restaurant/settings` needs the owner's current password (`currentPassword`; missing → 422 `REAUTH_REQUIRED`, wrong → 403 `REAUTH_FAILED`), checked with the sign-in scrypt and counted by the same per-email throttle (10 failures / 15 min → 429 `LOGIN_BLOCKED`, for sign-in too); rules-only changes need none. The bank confirm sheet on `/quan-ly/cai-dat` asks "Mật khẩu đăng nhập của bạn", shows a wrong password inline and never keeps it. `ConfirmSheet` now starts in its first form field when it has one.

### Added

- "Ẩn bàn": a table with history (that cannot be deleted) can be hidden from the refused-delete sheet — `POST /api/tables/:id/archive` / `unarchive` (owner only; 409 `TABLE_IN_USE` while guests sit at it), migration `0008_archive_tables` (`dining_tables.archived_at`); hidden tables leave the list (`GET /api/tables` → `{items, archived}`), the print sheet and booking assignment, their QR codes (current and old) answer "Không tìm thấy bàn", and "Bàn đã ẩn" lists them with "Hiện lại"; guest tokens resolve in one place (`TablesService.findLiveByToken`) (TQR-024).
- Kitchen "Bỏ món": `POST /api/kitchen/items/:id/cancel` (waiting or cooking → cancelled, owner and kitchen) with a confirm sheet on `/bep`; the guest sees "Đã huỷ" (TQR-025).

### Fixed

- A cancelled dish now re-syncs the table's open bill in the same transaction: the bill is superseded with the new amount (or no new bill when nothing is due) and the guest bill page updates live (TQR-025).
- `make bench` no longer fails when run right after other local traffic: setup requests that meet a per-address limit (429) wait for the window with backoff and retry, and the result reports `setup_429_retries` / `setup_429_wait_s`; production limits are unchanged (TQR-026).

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-08

Release 1 (local only): QR table ordering for many restaurants — sign-up and house rules, menu, tables and printable QR codes, staff roles, guest ordering with anomaly holds, fair kitchen queue, serving, live dish status, VietQR bills with cashier confirmation, online table booking, platform admin.

### Added

- A table that already paid a bill gets a new bill automatically when it orders more, shown live on an open bill page; the guest page also offers "Lấy hoá đơn mới" whenever money is due without an open bill (TQR-018).
- Tenant isolation sweep: every id-bearing staff endpoint in the role matrix is called by the owner of another restaurant and must answer 404 (28 endpoints, grows with the matrix) (TQR-017).
- Bill with VietQR: `bills` table (migration `0005_billing`, one open bill per session, code unique per restaurant), in-house EMVCo/NAPAS payload with CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE and a decoder (reproduces two published vectors byte for byte), 5-char Crockford bill codes, memo `TQR <code>`; a changed amount supersedes the old code ("Hoá đơn đã thay đổi"); no bank account → "Thanh toán tại quầy"; guest `GET/POST /api/g/bill` + live `/api/g/bill/stream` by the `tqr_guest` cookie; staff `POST /api/bills`, `GET /api/bills/:id`, `GET /api/sessions/:id/bill`; guest bill page `/t/:qrToken/hoa-don` (TQR-018).
- Cashier: `/thu-ngan` screen (replaces the placeholder) with open tables, totals, codes, "Đã nhận chuyển khoản" / "Đã nhận tiền mặt" through a confirm sheet repeating amount and code, closing with a required reason when unpaid, and the 24 h "Tài khoản nhận tiền vừa đổi" banner; `POST /api/bills/:id/pay` (compare-and-set, 409 `ALREADY_PAID`, audit log), `POST /api/sessions/:id/close`, `GET /api/bills/overview`; confirming payment releases pay-before-cooking orders with `queue_at` = confirmation time and publishes `item.status` (TQR-019).
- Staff booking list `/dat-ban` (owner, cashier, waiter; kitchen 403): day list with status chips and a "Trễ" chip 15 minutes after the start, confirm with table (re)assignment guarded by the same exclusion constraint, seat (opens a `table_sessions` row `opened_by='booking'` with the booked party size; 409 when the table already has an open session), cancel, no-show after the start; the owner's copyable public booking link; "Đặt bàn" in the owner navigation and on the cashier / waiter homes; guest name, phone and note erased 30 days after the slot by an hourly in-process job; `booking.status` realtime events on the floor topic (TQR-021).
- Guest table booking `/r/:slug/dat-ban`: start times from the opening hours and slot length (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh), party 1–30, Vietnamese mobile normalised to +84, note ≤ 140; holds the smallest free table that fits; Postgres `EXCLUDE USING gist` (`btree_gist`) makes simultaneous bookings of the last table end with exactly one winner; 409 `NO_TABLE_FREE` with the nearest free times, 422 `BOOKING_NOT_ALLOWED` with the reason (past, outside hours, closed day, booking off, > 60 days); `Idempotency-Key` retries return the same booking; at most 5 bookings per phone per restaurant in 24 h (`TOO_MANY_BOOKINGS`); `GET /api/g/r/:slug/slots`, `POST /api/g/r/:slug/bookings` (TQR-020).
- Guest check-in: scanning `/t/<qrToken>` asks the party size (1 … seats + 4) and opens the table, or joins its open session; "Vui lòng chờ nhân viên mở bàn" when staff open tables; `tqr_guest` device cookie (only its SHA-256 stored); staff `POST /api/sessions` and `PATCH /api/sessions/:id` (a new party size re-checks held orders) (TQR-011).
- Guest menu and cart with a sticky "Gửi món" bar and a note per dish; `POST /api/g/orders` with `Idempotency-Key`, server-side prices (`MENU_CHANGED` lists the reasons), a rules snapshot per order, released / held / pay-before statuses and kitchen events per station (TQR-012).

### Fixed

- Deleting a table no longer erases paid bills, sessions and bookings: a table with an open or past session or a booking answers 409 `TABLE_HAS_HISTORY` (checked under a row lock), and the foreign keys from `table_sessions` and `bookings` to `dining_tables` no longer cascade (migration `0007_restrict_table_delete`, `ON DELETE NO ACTION` so a restaurant delete still cascades); the confirm sheet says why ("Không xoá được Bàn 1") and offers "Đổi tên bàn" (TQR-007 review).
- Deleting a dish that guests ordered answers 409 `ITEM_HAS_ORDERS` instead of 500, and the sheet offers "Ngừng bán" (switches it to Hết món); the price field refuses "65000.5" or "1.5" (a dot only between groups of three digits); sheets keep their title and buttons fixed and scroll only the form, so "Xoá món" stays on screen at 390 px (TQR-006 review).
- One shell per role (r2 review): pages take their shell from `RoleShell` (owner → `OwnerShell` everywhere, floor screens included; others → `StaffShell`), navigation comes from one table (`ROLE_NAV`): owner Tổng quan · Thực đơn · Bàn & QR · Đặt bàn · Thêm on phones (Material 3, at most five) and the full rail with a "Vận hành" group (Thu ngân, Bếp, Phục vụ, Duyệt đơn) from 840 px; waiter Phục vụ · Duyệt đơn · Bàn & QR · Đặt bàn; cashier Thu ngân · Bàn & QR · Đặt bàn; kitchen none. The owner overview links to Thu ngân, Bếp and Phục vụ. A long restaurant name ends in "…" and no owner page scrolls sideways at 390 px any more (was 412–516 px); every Playwright journey on the iPhone project now fails on horizontal scroll (`e2e/support/fixtures.ts`).
- Settings: the field to fix is centred when focused, so the sticky save bar never covers it, and the bar sits flush above the bottom navigation.
- Consistent words and people (r2 review): sheets cancel with "Huỷ" ("Đóng" only where nothing changes); a person without a display name shows as "Chủ quán" (owner) or the email name, so the staff list shows the owner's email once (TQR-005/009); kitchen station tabs show how many dishes wait; the skip sheet says "Bàn này có một phần … gọi trước món này" when the earlier order is the same table (TQR-014).
- Guest bill entry: "Xem hoá đơn / Thanh toán" is the bottom bar's action once something is ordered (cart empty), and the pay-before banner's "Thanh toán tại quầy hoặc bằng mã QR" is a link to the bill; the guest never has to type `/hoa-don` (TQR-018 AC40).
- An open bill follows new items live: the transaction that releases or bills new items (guest order released or put to payment, waiter confirming a held order, AC20 re-check, a payment that leaves money due) supersedes the open bill and issues the new code, publishing `bill.status` on `session:<id>`; the open guest bill page shows "Hoá đơn đã thay đổi" with the new amount and QR without a reload (`billing/bill-sync.ts`, TQR-018 AC42).
- Pay-before orders leave "Chờ thanh toán" live after payment: the release publishes `order.status`, and the guest page applies `order.status` and `bill.status` (paid) events to the order cards (TQR-016).
- Throttles on the public paths (in memory, per process, `RequestLimits`): 20 orders / 10 min per guest phone (429 `TOO_MANY_ORDERS`, the cart stays), 6 bill requests / min per phone, 30 check-ins / min and 10 public bookings / hour per client address (429 `TOO_MANY_REQUESTS`), each with a `TQR_*` setting (TQR-011, TQR-012, TQR-018, TQR-020).
- Guest and staff live streams draw from separate per-restaurant pools (`TQR_STREAMS_PER_RESTAURANT` staff, `TQR_GUEST_STREAMS_PER_RESTAURANT` guests, default 600), so guest phones can no longer lock the kitchen and floor screens out (TQR-010).
- Check-in asks `GET /api/g/check-in?qr=` first (join / party size / wait for staff), so loading the check-in page no longer logs a 409 (TQR-011).
- Hold reasons: an order without main dishes never gets the anomaly reason (a drinks order under "every order" says "Quán xác nhận mọi đơn"); the anomaly reason now shows the session's main portions against the limit for the guest and the waiter (`sessionMainPortions`, `maxMainPerPerson` in the order views) (TQR-013).
- Cashier: a payment confirmed by someone without a display name shows "Chủ quán" for the owner, else the email's local part (resolved by the API, same rule as the staff list), instead of "Chuyển khoản · · 11:24"; a 409 on "Đã nhận" closes the stale sheet and shows the reason over the refreshed list (TQR-019).
- Cancel buttons of the waiter's reject sheet and the staff booking sheets read "Huỷ" (was "Quay lại").
- Guest booking: changing the party size keeps the chosen time when it is still free, otherwise it says "Giờ … không còn bàn cho … khách" and asks for a new time instead of silently picking another (TQR-020).
- Tests: VietQR CRC below 0x1000 (`…630400AF`, 4-digit zero padding); a phone of table A scanning table B gets its own device and its A cookie never reads or orders in B; guest order and bill streams end when the cashier closes the table; guest delivery freshness (30 tables × 3 phones + 2 floor screens) (TQR-018, TQR-011, TQR-016).
- `make bench` measures the real "guest submits a 5-line cart" workload (k6 setup builds a 40-item restaurant, 30 tables and checked-in phones through the API); `make lighthouse` with `LH_GUEST_MENU=1` also measures a seeded 40-item guest menu page (`guest-menu-cold-load`) (TQR-012, TQR-016).
- One shared `IconButton` replaces the menu's three hand-rolled icon-button styles; one queued `Toast` (`ToastRegion` in both shells + `useToastStore`, polite live region) replaces the page-local success notices (settings, tables, admin, staff list, staff detail, and after the merge the cashier, bookings and serving screens) and gives the menu page success feedback; the menu page's header actions stack full width on phones like tables and staff (TQR-006, TQR-008).
- Floor staff on "Bàn & QR" no longer see owner navigation or print buttons that bounce them: the print sheet is open to owner, cashier and waiter, the owner-area navigation lists only pages the role can open, and the waiter / cashier homes link to "Bàn & QR" (TQR-007).
- Menu caps per restaurant: 20 stations, 50 categories, 500 items → 409 `STATION_LIMIT_REACHED` / `CATEGORY_LIMIT_REACHED` / `ITEM_LIMIT_REACHED`, creation serialised per restaurant; the staff and guest menu reads stay bounded by them (TQR-006).
- AC54 guest half: `GET /api/g/t/:token` and `GET /api/g/r/:slug/menu` answer 403 `RESTAURANT_SUSPENDED` for a suspended restaurant (was 404) and the guest table page now really shows "Nhà hàng tạm ngưng" (TQR-009).
- The AC15 role matrix now covers the 12 staff menu endpoints and the floor stream (status only), and a test proves a guarded route without `@Roles` refuses every role (TQR-005, TQR-006, TQR-010).
- Open live streams end when the person is disabled, gets a new password or role, or the restaurant is suspended (`tqr_revoke` NOTIFY in the same transaction, plus a session re-check on every heartbeat); every stream (floor, kitchen station, guest order status, guest bill) opens through one `StreamGate`: capped at 10 per staff member, 30 per guest table session and 300 per restaurant per process (429 `TOO_MANY_STREAMS`), guest streams end on suspension or when the phone's session link is gone; snapshot failures and dropped malformed NOTIFY payloads are logged without content (TQR-010).
- Sign-in throttle (AC4) holds under parallel requests: each attempt is reserved under a per-email advisory lock before the password check; unknown and disabled accounts are checked against a dummy scrypt hash so timing does not reveal which emails exist; a failed sign-in no longer signs the caller out (rotation only on success); sign-up and sign-in are limited to 20 requests / minute per client address (`TQR_AUTH_RATE_LIMIT_PER_MINUTE`, 429 `TOO_MANY_REQUESTS`) and at most 4 scrypt computations run at once (TQR-004).
- A session cookie with malformed percent-encoding is treated as absent (401) instead of a 500 (TQR-004).
